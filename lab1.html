About CivicSetu

Civic grievance systems generate large amounts of historical data. Looking at complaints individually may make recurring patterns difficult to identify, especially across wards and categories.

CivicSetu uses data analysis and AI-assisted pattern detection to identify recurring issues, persistent problem areas, and emerging trends. The system is designed to support better municipal planning, faster prioritization, and evidence-based decision making for civic authorities.

  • Recurring issue detection
  • Ward-level analysis
  • Grievance pattern analysis
  • Data-driven decision support
